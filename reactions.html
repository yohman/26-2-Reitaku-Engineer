Reitaku Engineering · Fall 2026

Listen.
Think again.

Reaction papers turn a guest’s experience into your own question.

Purpose

Go beyond a summary.

Identify a specific idea from the talk, explain why it mattered to you, and show how it changed or sharpened your thinking.

These are suggested prompts. Submission method, deadline, length, and grading details will be announced in class.

01

What stood out?

Choose one experience or idea and explain why.

02

What changed?

Which assumption did it challenge?

03

Where does society enter?

Consider the people affected by an engineering choice.

04

What would you ask next?

Pose a question for the speaker or for your own investigation.
