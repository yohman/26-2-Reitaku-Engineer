## Bio

Alesha Unpingco is a multidisciplinary interaction designer, user experience architect, and builder at Niantic Spatial. Her work explores how emerging technologies can create useful, engaging experiences. Earlier in her career, she worked on immersive technology and user experience at Google, following design work for clients including Honda, Acura, and Intuit. She studied English at UCLA.
