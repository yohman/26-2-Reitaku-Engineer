## Bio

Yasuo Usuba is CPO at LocationMind, a technology company originating from the University of Tokyo’s Shibasaki Laboratory. The company works on mobility data analysis, visualization, AI prediction, and secure positioning. After graduating from the University of Tokyo’s Faculty of Engineering, he worked at Recruit, IBM, and GE Consumer Finance, and later led channel sales in Japan at Google. He holds an MBA from the University of Rochester and joined LocationMind in March 2021.

[LocationMind — company and team](https://locationmind.com/)
