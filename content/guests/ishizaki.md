## Bio

At the time of the 2011 disaster, Ishizaki served as TEPCO executive vice president and later represented its Fukushima Revitalization Headquarters.

## Bio JP

2011年の震災当時、東京電力の副社長を務め、その後福島復興本社の代表を務めました。
