## Bio

Albert S. Kochaphum works at the intersection of mapping and social justice, with a strong emphasis on community-based mapping. His profile lists UCLA and highlights experience in GIS, urban research, and public-interest mapping. His work connects spatial data with the needs and knowledge of the communities represented on maps.
