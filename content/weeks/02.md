## Discussion

After the film, take four minutes to write down one moment or contrast that stayed with you. Then form groups of four; a few groups will have three. Let everyone speak once before the open discussion.
Which perspective stayed with you? What can AI contribute to human expression, and what might it fail to replace? Where did your group agree or disagree? Point to something in the film when explaining your view.
Each group: post one question your conversation raised in the [live question app](https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbz7kuUplBbrLkwNnpCSfE_fH3Ua1PL3rd3Ml84l4-oc13gfjQmDaCY1OF4AkGNyiwEF/exec?lecture=engineer). Begin with your group number, such as G03. Prepare to share one key point and one unresolved question in two minutes; a few groups will report to the class.

## Discussion JP

鑑賞後、心に残った場面や対比を一つ、まず4分間で各自メモしてください。その後、4人組（一部は3人組）で話し合います。最初は全員が一度ずつ発言しましょう。
誰の視点が心に残りましたか。AIは人間の表現に何をもたらし、何を置き換えられないでしょうか。意見が一致した点・分かれた点は何ですか。映画の場面に触れながら考えてください。
各グループは、話し合いから生まれた問いを[質問アプリ](https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbz7kuUplBbrLkwNnpCSfE_fH3Ua1PL3rd3Ml84l4-oc13gfjQmDaCY1OF4AkGNyiwEF/exec?lecture=engineer)に一つ投稿してください。冒頭に「G03」などグループ番号を付けます。大事な話題と残った問いを2分で共有できるように準備してください。数グループが全体に発表します。

## Reaction

At the end of class, write one idea you changed or sharpened after hearing another group, and one question you are still considering.
