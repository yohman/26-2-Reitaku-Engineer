## Activity

- Choose a goal and describe one person’s problem in a sentence (7 minutes).
- Read it to 2–3 people nearby, answer their questions, and make it more specific (5 minutes).
- Ask AI for a plan, build one working feature, and improve one part (20 minutes).
- Let a neighbor try it without explaining the controls. Ask where they hesitated, whether it helps, and who might be left out (5 minutes).

## Activity JP

- 選ぶ：目標を一つ選び、ひとりの困りごとを一文にする（7分）。
- 話す：近くの2〜3人に一文を読み、質問を受けて具体的に書き直す（5分）。
- 作る：AIにまず計画を頼み、動く機能を一つつくり、1か所直す（20分）。
- 見せる：使い方を説明せず、隣の人に触ってもらう。「どこで迷った？」「効きそう？」「取りこぼされる人は？」を聞く（5分）。

## Submission

Submit a prototype card through UNIPA: your SDG number and theme island; the problem in one sentence; a screenshot and URL if available; one observation from the person who tried it; someone who could be left out; and what you want to explore next. The cards will inform future groups of 4–6 students working on related themes.

## Submission JP
